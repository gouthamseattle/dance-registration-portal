Privacy

Last updated October 2026.

What we collect

When you register for a GouMo Dance Chronicles class we collect your name, email address, Instagram handle, dance experience, the classes you sign up for and how you paid (Venmo or Zelle). We do not collect or store bank or card details; payments happen in your Venmo or Zelle app.

How we use it

  • To manage your registration, check payments and keep class sizes right.
  • To email you about your registration: confirmations, cancellations and waitlist spots.

We send these emails from our own Gmail account using Google's Gmail API. We only use it to send messages; the website does not read your email or anyone's mailbox.

Sharing

We don't sell or share your information. Only the GouMo team can see it.

Your choices

To update or delete your information, email us at goumo.dancechronicles@gmail.com or message @goumo_dancechronicles on Instagram.
